- Helped scale Attio's engineering org through conducting **100s of interviews**, leading our build system initiative as well as numerous CI (linting, formatting, testing) and CD (deployments, versioning) aspects along with observability (errors, performance monitoring & analytics) and Agile (planning, retros).
