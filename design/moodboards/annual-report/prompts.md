# Annual-report homepage moodboards

<!-- cspell:words glassmorphism grotesk moodboard moodboards -->

Generated with the built-in image generation workflow using the current homepage screenshot as
a visual reference rather than an edit target.

## Shared brief

- Produce a 3:2 landscape website-redesign moodboard with a dominant desktop concept, a clear
  employer-interaction state, typography and palette details, and (where useful) a mobile view.
- Preserve the recognizable dithered portrait, tactile paper, and spectral bottom-edge colour as
  the site's signature material.
- Replace the current equally weighted prose with a decisive annual-report hierarchy: one claim,
  one image, strict alignment, and small factual metadata.
- Keep the employer disclosures, but recast them as a print-native interaction such as tabs,
  report notes, tracing-paper callouts, expandable rows, contact sheets, or divider inserts.
- Avoid browser chrome, unrelated people, company logos, watermarks, glassmorphism, rounded SaaS
  cards, generic dashboards, decorative nostalgia, and lorem ipsum.
- Treat all generated panel prose and metrics as visual placeholders, not publishable biography
  copy.

## 01 / Chairman's Statement

Late-1960s corporate annual report on warm ivory stock. Use a strict asymmetric 12-column grid,
a narrow black index spine, huge quiet paper, a hard-cropped lower-right portrait, an oversized
neutral-grotesk claim, book-serif statement, and tiny tabular metadata. Limit ink to brown-black,
vermilion, and cyan. Turn the spectral footer into a printer proof strip. Make employers numbered
marginal tabs and show one flat “note to the report” sliding from the margin without shifting the
portrait.

Key copy: `BRADEN MARSHALL`, `PRODUCT ENGINEER — LONDON`, `REPORT 26`,
`I BUILD USEFUL SYSTEMS.`, `SELECTED WORK`, `01 ISEMBARD`, `02 ATTIO`,
`03 THOUGHT MACHINE`, `ROLE / PERIOD / OUTCOME`.

## 02 / Distributed Works

Late-1970s industrial annual report and engineering drawing set on slightly yellowed stock. Use a
bold 35/65 split, deep blueprint-navy field, safety orange and mineral blue, compressed industrial
grotesk type, and a giant `~100×` statistic. Cross the dithered portrait with a meaningful exploded
process diagram linking software, systems, people, and factory output. Turn employers into
numbered schematic leaders; open Isembard as a translucent tracing-paper annotation with
`ROLE / SYSTEM / OUTPUT / LESSON`. Convert the footer spectrum into a calibration ruler.

## 03 / Human Scale

Elegant mid-1980s report using documentary imagery, contact sheets, captions, and radical crops.
Make a huge duotone crop of Braden's face and hands the main field, supported by two small contact
frames and a narrow caption band. Use bone, photographic black, aubergine, dusty rose, and mustard;
pair a high-contrast serif with condensed captions and tiny mono metadata. Make employers numbered
contact-sheet frames; show Attio selected with a spot-colour border and a flat editorial caption
card containing period, role, and change.

Key copy: `THE WORK IS TECHNICAL.`, `THE OUTCOME IS HUMAN.`,
`NOTES ON BUILDING USEFUL THINGS.`, `CONTACT SHEET / 01—05`.

## 04 / A Working Index

Disciplined early-1990s statistical appendix and institutional chronology. Let large tabular years,
rules, folios, and aligned disclosure rows become the hero; reduce the portrait to a circular seal
and pale oversized watermark. Use paper white, black, institutional green, cobalt, and fluorescent
chartreuse. Expand the Attio row inside the ledger with a restrained image strip and columns for
`OUTCOME / METRIC / LESSON`; show an equally clear stacked mobile state. Turn the spectral footer
into a labelled data legend.

Key rows: `2026—NOW ISEMBARD`, `2020—2026 ATTIO`, `2019—2020 THOUGHT MACHINE`,
`2017—2019 THEODO`, `2014—2017 CARDIFF`, `2013—2014 SELF-EMPLOYED`.

## 05 / Afterimage

Adventurous late-1990s technology report on charcoal with bone inserts and translucent fluorescent
overprints. Mask an off-centre negative dithered portrait through fragments of huge wide-geometric
words, surrounded by tiny OCR metadata. Lift the current footer spectrum into one narrow vertical
beam that becomes the layout axis. Stack employer names as fluorescent report-divider tabs behind
the portrait and fan the selected Attio insert outward with `MANDATE / WHAT CHANGED /
WHAT I LEARNED`. Include a three-frame `REST / PRESS / RELEASE` study of the reactive dot field.

Key copy: `BUILD / DISTRIBUTE / COMPOUND`, `PRODUCT ENGINEER — LONDON`,
`SELECTED SYSTEMS`, `01 ISEMBARD`, `02 ATTIO`, `03 THOUGHT MACHINE`.
